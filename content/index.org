I'm Dalton Allen. I asked for an Excel book for my sixth birthday, and
the spreadsheet thing never wore off. These days I'm a fractional CFO
in Los Angeles, running finance for nonprofits that have outgrown
their bookkeeper but aren't ready for a full-time CFO. That covers
strategy, budgeting, reporting, automation, and the operations that
hold it together. The goal is always the same: numbers that work for
the mission instead of the other way around. If that sounds like your
organization, [[#contact][send me a note]].

I started in public and corporate accounting, became Director of
Finance for a 100-person law firm at 24, and was CFO of a California
wine startup at 28, where we grew revenue from $18M to $100M. Then I
opened my own practice. Since then I've worked across a lot of
industries, including a stint as interim CEO of a venture-funded
health startup.

* Why nonprofits
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: why-nonprofits
:END:

I spent my early career in for-profit and startup companies. At some
point I wanted my energy, in and outside of work, going toward things
that do good in the world. So I pivoted to nonprofits.

My first nonprofit client taught me something I didn't expect:
nonprofit accounting is stuck in the past. The sector runs a decade
behind its for-profit counterparts on the best accounting technology,
by my count. Nonprofits see cost as the barrier. The real barrier is
initiative. When I brought in the same tools the for-profits already
use, I could offer a much higher level of service, CFO work where they
had been getting bookkeeping, without any increase in budget.

* How I work
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: how-i-work
:END:

- Correct first. A model that is fast and wrong is worse than no model.
- Legible to the next person. If the board treasurer can't follow the
  spreadsheet, it isn't finished.
- Automate the boring parts so the interesting conversations get the
  time.
- Big picture and small details are the same job. I like the strategy
  conversation, and I'm just as happy three tabs deep in a workbook.
