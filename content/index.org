I'm Dalton Allen, a fractional CFO in Los Angeles. I run finance for
nonprofits that have outgrown their bookkeeper but aren't ready for a
full-time CFO: strategy, budgeting, reporting, automation, and the
operations that hold it together. The goal is always the same: numbers
that work for the mission instead of the other way around. If that
sounds like your organization, [[#contact][send me a note]].

I've been at home in finance for most of my life. I started in public
and corporate accounting, became Director of Finance for a 100-person
law firm at 24, and was CFO of a California wine startup at 28, where
we grew revenue from $18M to $100M. Then I opened my own practice, and
since then I've worked across a lot of industries, including a stint
as interim CEO of a venture-funded health startup.

* How I work
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: how-i-work
:END:

- Correct first. A model that is fast and wrong is worse than no model.
- Legible to the next person. If the board treasurer can't follow the
  spreadsheet, it isn't finished.
- Automate the boring parts so the interesting conversations get the
  time.
- Big picture and small details are the same job. I like the strategy
  conversation, and I'm just as happy three tabs deep in a workbook.
